New Frontier, tour sales for Patagonia hotels

Figures as of November 2026

Validated a tour-sales tool with real data and pivoted from a tourist app to a B2B tool before writing any code.

Role
Product Owner (with a partner who works in tourism in Patagonia)
Timeline
2026
Stack
To be defined with the developer

The problem

Hotels and agencies near Torres del Paine sell tours through WhatsApp, paper and spreadsheets. Commissions are calculated by hand, mistakes happen and there is no record of who sold what.

Discovery

The first idea was an app for tourists to book tours directly. Interviews with agencies showed that hotels are the main booking channel, so cutting them out was not realistic. We pivoted to a tool for the hotels and agencies themselves. I validated the commission structure with six months of real sales data from a partner in the industry and benchmarked market commissions (typically 15 to 20%). Before defining the MVP, I sent the domain expert a structured set of questions on workflow, pain points, master data, commission rules, pilot and confidentiality.

Key decisions

  • ▹Pivot on evidence, not preference. The B2C idea was dropped because the data said so.
  • ▹Build once, reuse many times. The tool will be the first template of a multi-tenant platform, Bizmonkey, so other small businesses (a second one is already interested) can run their own site on the same base, with admin and collaborator roles.
  • ▹Name checked before committing. Platform names were checked for availability before choosing one.

What I built

  • ▹A validated problem and commission model, an approved design direction, and a product brief with MVP scope, out-of-scope items and roles, ready for a developer.

Status and results

Concept, ready for development.

What’s next

Build the MVP (sales log, extras, automatic commission calculation, KPI dashboard, roles) and confirm a pilot hotel.
